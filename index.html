HOLLOW PINES

A night in the dead woods

Your truck died on the old fire road and the forest swallowed the light. Five ranger signal beacons still stand out there — relight them all and the lookout will see you. Find the fuel. Keep the beam low. Something walks these pines, and it listens.

The woods hold their breath.
